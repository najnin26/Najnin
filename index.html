03 Biography

I am a Senior Research Assistant at ELITE Research Lab and a Lecturer at Uttara University, where my research focuses on Quantum Neural Networks, Variational Quantum Circuits (VQCs), and Quantum NLP, leveraging superposition, entanglement, and interference to design learning systems that exceed classical limits.

My work spans the full AI stack — from parameterized quantum circuits for classification and optimization, to quantum-inspired models in NLP and Computer Vision. I am particularly interested in how hybrid classical–quantum systems can unlock new efficiencies in machine learning. My broader interests include Large Language Models, Graph Signal Processing, and Data Science.
